# Standout sites: what they look like (Desktop Chrome pass, 2026-11-02)

The Laptop's ask on #1: `genre-and-standouts.md` section 2 read these as HTML text, which shows neither type nor
color. This is the visual pass, desktop ~1568px, first screen only. Screenshots: `shots/standouts/<name>.jpg`.
Judged against the owner's question: what is actually more eye-appealing, and what can Gabe's site take from it.

## What this adds to the direction
1. **Name-as-hero with a photo in the type (Kalkbrenner)** is the most eye-appealing, cheapest-to-build idea in the
   whole research set, and no peer in Gabe's lane does it. Proposed hero: "GLUCAK" huge in a condensed display face,
   a live photo (or a muted, slow, pause-able clip) inside a gap in the word, the slogan on the line under it, Listen /
   Shows buttons, the Now strip. The player starts right below, visible on the first phone screen.
2. **One saturated era color + black on duotoned live photos (Inherited)** confirms the IDENTITY era system works for
   heavy, high-energy music. Covers stay as made (Laptop's correction).
3. **Album as a world (Dookie, Karma)** belongs on the IDENTITY page, not Home: the 12 tracks as cards, filled in as
   singles drop, Scenic Route last.
4. **Cover strip (Lady Gaga)**: a compact row of release covers as the discography on Music.
5. **Confirmed don'ts:** glitch / distortion video (Gaga), tiny low-contrast type and clever nav (Alcest), loaders and
   WebGL (Karma).
