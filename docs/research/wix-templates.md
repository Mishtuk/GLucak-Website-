## What the templates get right (keep)
1. One idea per screen: the hero is one headline + one or two actions. Gabe's slogan fits this slot.
2. Giant type does the energy work (jaxx-lumiere, joey-lante, lunar-flux), no motion needed: ideal for the
   no-flashing rule.
3. Duotone / single-color photography makes one photo shoot look like a brand, and recolors per era.
4. Listen links (platform icons) next to the main CTA; a player in the first screen (rap-artist-events).
5. Sign-up framed as joining something ("Join the movement / frequency") rather than "newsletter".

## What they get wrong (beat this)
1. **Generic copy and stock photos**: every hero line could be any artist ("Ride the Sonic Wave"). Gabe has a real
   slogan and story; use them.
2. **Nav hides the two main actions**: several miss Music or Shows from the nav. Ours: Listen and Shows always visible.
3. **Too long on phones** (lunar-flux 10k px, jaxx 6k px): sections repeat. Ours: Home under ~5 phone screens.
4. **Built-in Wix players and event apps** instead of where fans already listen (Spotify) and buy tickets.
5. **No era system**: colors are fixed; a new album means a redesign. Ours swaps an era in one file.
6. **Log In buttons** on every header for fans who have no account: clutter. Skip.
