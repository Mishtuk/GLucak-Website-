# Email thread with Gabe, Nov 1-13, 2026: requests and promises (digest for the overhaul)

Source: the owner's email thread "A few questions before I start on your website" (Nov 1 -> Nov 13), read 2026-11-18. Email
addresses, phone numbers and pricing are deliberately left out (public repo). Gabe's exact answers to the question lists are in
`README.md` (Nov 2) and `docs/research/gabe-answers-2026-11-06.md` (Nov 6); this file adds the owner's promises and the Nov 13 email.

## What Gabe asked for (his words, Nov 2)
- "A central hub for my music where anyone can go and find out the latest": **new releases, upcoming shows, the latest videos, and
  maybe a connection to get merch**. "Essentially, I want to centralize where things are."
- Main actions: **listen to the music and come to the shows**; merch too. Audience: **largely fans**.
- Wants: **music player, videos, photo gallery, upcoming shows, booking or contact form, merch / music store, email sign-up**,
  plus a **Patreon link and a bio**. Not lessons.
- **Spotify playlist player near the top**, embedded, "not just a hyperlink" (like Thousand Foot Krutch did).
- **Slogan featured prominently**: "Rise, Live It Loud, Push Past Fear, The Time Is Now."
- Feel: **"Intuitive, Versatile and just straight-up Cool."** Likes Manafest's site and Riverside's layout; dislikes Puck Live's
  ("harder to find ... not where you expect them ... not always up to date").
- Colours follow the release being promoted; IDENTITY colours first.
- Start with 3-4 sections and add as time goes; shop later but set up in the background.
- **Small changes himself, big ones sent to us.** Changes come with releases and tours.
- Shared a **Discord invite** with his answers (not on the site yet; ask before linking it publicly).

## What the owner promised Gabe (owner's emails)
- **Nov 3 (proposal email):** a site "built from scratch for GLucak, not a template". The slogan runs down Home as the section
  headings: **Rise** (Spotify playlist near the top + latest release), **Live It Loud** (upcoming shows + latest video; past shows
  move to "Played" by themselves), **Push Past Fear** (his story + Patreon), **The Time Is Now** (email sign-up + IDENTITY countdown
  with pre-save once dated). The look is built around IDENTITY and **a new release's colours and art swap in as one quick change**.
  **"A simple edit screen to add shows, update release links and change your bio yourself."** Launch: Home, Music, Shows, About +
  web address, matching email address, email list; shop set up behind the scenes. Accounts in his name; the owner helps manage them.
- **Nov 5:** offered to walk Gabe through the design (screenshots or a call) "before I build the rest"; sharper frames if he has the
  original videos.
- **Nov 6:** "your real Spotify playlist plays right on the page, every release shows with its cover, fans can add your shows to
  their calendar", the Now line ("Out Nov 13: Who I Am"); a **private edit screen with its own sign-in** where he adds shows and
  downloads a backup; a shop ready to switch on; a booking address on his domain forwarding to his Gmail. Go-live target: **week of
  January 11**.
