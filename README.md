# GLucak-Website-
A Music website/Shop

The following is an email with the client that should serve as context for what the client wants from the website and what should be referenced when making the website
The Big Picture



1. In a sentence or two, what do you want this website to be? I'm assuming it will center on your music, but tell me if you have something else in mind.



Yes, I’m looking to create a central hub for my music where anyone can go and find out the latest what’s going on (specifically New releases, upcoming shows, the latest videos, and maybe a connection to get merch from). Essentially, I want to centralize where things are.



2. What's the main thing you want visitors to do? For example: listen to your music, book you, buy something, or get in touch.



I’m going to say… it’s kind of 2 things in one. Listen to the music and come to the shows. Merch can be put in there too, but that’s kind of the main idea.



3. Who are you most trying to reach? For example: fans, venues, event planners, students, or people in the music industry.



Largely fans.



You and Your Music



4. How would you describe what you do? Genre, instruments, and whether you're solo or in a group are all helpful, as is whether you perform, write, produce, or teach.



Like I tend to put in my bio, I consider myself an independent producer-rapper, but it’s a little more complicated; that’s the simplified version. I write and produce everything, do the vocals, and release it all pretty much independently, although I definitely enjoy linking up with people. As far as genre, I’ve moved into a position where I’m an EDM, Rap and Rock tri-hybrid. The lyrics are always raw and faith-based, usually drawing on real-life experience and circumstances. 



I’m both a recording and performing artist, all high energy stuff. I sometimes perform with other artists, predominantly the guitarist Fran Carroll in recent days, but at the end of the day, I’m a solo artist. I do a lot of open mic performances in the area as well, and a few full-length shows, hoping to eventually do more of the latter.



5. What should people know about you and your story? If you already have a bio, please send it over.



I do have a current bio on Spotify, but am planning to change it as soon as January hits because I have a third album (called IDENTITY) coming soon, and do a new bio each time a major release is cycling in. I’ll put the current one here, and send the new one at a later date.



“Despite facing various difficulties and struggles over the years, Epilepsy survivor and Producer-Rapper/DJ GLucak has shown no intent to give up on either his Faith in God or his mission in creating energetic music to bring others to Christ. Two albums and numerous singles and EPs into the biz and with no plans to slow down, GLucak has been running at a ferocious pace crafting brand-new content and music for 2026 and 2027. Nevertheless, no matter the musical style or genre, be it his debut single or his upcoming 3rd album (rumored to be his best work yet), the mission statement has always been the same: Rise, Live It Loud, Push Past Fear, The Time Is Now.”
